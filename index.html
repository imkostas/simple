// 01. DOSSIER

Kostas Terzidis

Professor, D&I, Tongji University
Director, ShangXiang Lab

Dr. Kostas Terzidis is an academic pioneer exploring the friction points between computation, architecture, and artificial intelligence. Currently commanding the ShangXiang Lab at Tongji University, his ongoing inquiries challenge the deterministic boundaries of software-aided design.

His distinguished academic trajectory includes positions as Associate Professor at the Harvard University Graduate School of Design (2003–2011) and Assistant Professor at UCLA (1995–2003). He holds a PhD from the University of Michigan, a Master's from OSU, and a diploma from Aristotle University.

Current Research Focus: AI & Art, pedagogical frameworks for youth AI literacy, international AI+Art symposia, and philosophical papers on algorithmic intentionality.
