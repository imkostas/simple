01. Persona & Legacy

Kostas Terzidis is a pioneer in algorithmic architecture and generative design. Having taught at UCLA and Harvard GSD for over a decade, his work delves deep into the computational logic of visual form.

He is the sole author of four foundational texts in the field: Permutation Design (Routledge: 2014), Algorithms for Visual Design (Wiley: 2009), Algorithmic Architecture (Architectural Press: 2006), and Expressive Form (Spon: 2003).

His current focus centers on Artificial Intelligence and Art, establishing conferences, courses for kids & AI, and groundbreaking theoretical research into algorithmic intentionality.

02. The Concept of Diapathy

A conceptual framework formulated by Kostas Terzidis exploring the nuanced space between empathy, apathy, and artificial consciousness in generative design.

"Between machine indifference and human emotional projection lies 'Diapathy'—the generative synthesis of artificial agency and architectural intuition."
— ShangXiang Lab Research
