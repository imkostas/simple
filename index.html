// SUBJECT DOSSIER

Kostas Terzidis

Professor, D&I, Tongji University
Director, ShangXiang Lab

An operative at the threshold of computation and creative expression. Dr. Kostas Terzidis currently commands direction over the ShangXiang Lab at Tongji University. His intellectual trajectory traces a calculated path across prime institutions of design automation.

Before vanishing into the Eastern theatre of AI developments, his positions included an Associate Professorship at the Harvard University Graduate School of Design (2003-2011) and an Assistant Professorship at UCLA (1995-2003). He operates equipped with a PhD from the University of Michigan, a Master's from Ohio State University, and a foundational diploma from Aristotle University.
